# VyaaparSathi 🇮🇳

**VyaaparSathi** is a digital platform designed to help small businesses and local shop owners manage and grow their businesses using simple, accessible technology.

## 🚀 About the Project

VyaaparSathi aims to bridge the gap between traditional small businesses and modern digital tools. The platform provides useful features that can help shop owners manage their day-to-day operations, understand their business better, and make smarter decisions.

## 🎯 Problem

Many small and local businesses still depend on manual processes for managing their products, sales, customers, and business information. They may also find existing business software complicated or difficult to use.

VyaaparSathi focuses on providing a **simple, user-friendly, and practical solution** for these businesses.

## ✨ Key Features

* 📦 Product and inventory management
* 💰 Sales and transaction tracking
* 📊 Business insights and analytics
* 👥 Customer management
* 🤖 AI-assisted business support
* 📱 Simple and accessible user interface
* 📈 Tools to help businesses make better decisions

## 🛠️ Technologies

* **Frontend:** React / TypeScript
* **Backend:** Node.js / Express
* **Database:** Supabase
* **AI:** Gemini API
* **Development:** Git & GitHub

## 🌱 Future Improvements

* Multilingual support for local business owners
* Voice-based business assistance
* AI-powered sales and demand predictions
* Digital payment integration
* WhatsApp-based business assistance
* Advanced business analytics

## 👨‍💻 Project Goal

The goal of VyaaparSathi is to make digital business tools **simple, affordable, and accessible to small businesses**, helping them adopt technology without needing advanced technical knowledge.
